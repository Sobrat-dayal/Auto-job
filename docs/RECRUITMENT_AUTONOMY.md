# AUTONOM Recruitment Autonomy Modes

## Stage 25 of 50 — Operational Autonomy Boundaries

---

### The 4 Autonomy Modes

| Mode | Title | Capabilities | Human Involvement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mode 1** | Research Only | Read-only discovery and matching. | Zero execution. Agents only produce research digests. |
| **Mode 2** | Prepare & Review *(Default)* | Scouting, tailoring resumes, drafting cover letters, preparing answers. | **100% human sign-off**. Every application must be approved before submission. |
| **Mode 3** | Preauthorized Preparation | Continuous background preparation under scoped standing grants. | Zero interruptions during preparation. Review occurs at submission stage. |
| **Mode 4** | Bounded Autonomous Applications | Autonomous submission strictly within daily budgets, 100% invariant passes. | Pre-authorized boundaries. Emergency stop can halt at any moment. |

---

### Safety Guarantees

Under no mode will AUTONOM:
- Bypass missing work authorization attestations.
- Submit answers to sensitive questions (clearance, criminal background, non-competes) without explicit user attestations.
- Exceed multi-agent shared daily application caps.
- Continue operating when Emergency Stop (Stage 14) is engaged.
